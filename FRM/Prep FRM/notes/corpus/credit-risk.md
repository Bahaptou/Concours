Credit risk refers to a loss suffered by a party whereby the **counterparty fails to meet its contractual obligations.** Credit risk may arise if there is an increasing **risk of default** by the counterparty throughout the duration of the contract. 
There are four subtypes of credit risk: 
- [[default-risk]]
- [[bankruptcy-risk]]
- [[downgrade-risk]]
- [[counterparty-risk]]
