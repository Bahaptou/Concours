Equity risk is the risk of loss because of a drop in the market price of shares. **(volatility)** 

Equity risk arises from **fluctuations in the share price** and the risk of the **issuer defaulting**. It is considered as a [[market-risk]].

Equity risk often refers to **equity investment risk**, which is the risk associated with the investment in shares/stocks of a company due to **company-specific factors** (e.g., rising cost of inputs, strategic weaknesses, etc.). In this case the equity risk is a [[specific-risk]].

Market risk cannot be diversified away, while specific risk can be mitigated by holding assets with less than perfect correlations.
