Market risk is the risk that results due to **movements in market prices and rates.**

It contains four key subtypes :
- [[interest-rate-risk]]
- [[equity-risk]]
- [[foreign-exchange-risk]] / [[currency-risk]]
- [[commodity-price-risk]]

The key to mitigating these risks is to understand the relationship between positions. As these relationships change, risk management methods need to change as well.
