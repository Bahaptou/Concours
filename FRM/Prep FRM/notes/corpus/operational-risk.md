Operational risk refers to the risk of loss resulting from inadequate or failed :
- Internal processes
- People and systems
- External events. 

Operational risks include **aml** risk, **cyber** risk, and **rogue trading.**

Moreover, operational include corporate disasters such as **operational mishaps and corporate governance scandals**. It also includes **natural disasters.**
