A probability mass function (PMF), f (x) = P(X = x), gives us the probability that the outcome of a [[discrete-random-variable]], X, will be equal to a given number, x.

example of a PMF is f(x) = 1/6, which is the probability that one roll of a six-sided die will take on one of the possible outcomes one through six. Each of the possible outcomes has the same probability of occurring (1/6 = 16.67%).
