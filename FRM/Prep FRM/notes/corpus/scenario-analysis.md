Scenario analysis is **a process that considers potential future risk factors and the associated alternative outcomes**. 

The typical method is to **compare a best-case scenario to a worst-case scenario**, which shocks variables to their **extreme known values.** This process factors in the potential impact of several categories of risk and influences risk manager decision making by attempting to **put a value on an otherwise qualitative concept (i.e., what-if analysis).** This exercise is an attempt to understand the **severity of potential losses rather than the frequency of losses.**

It is a [[qualitative-risk-assessment]]
